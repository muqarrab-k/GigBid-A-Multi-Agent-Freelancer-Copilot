# Dental Clinic Expansion Model

In late 2022, I worked with **Sunrise Dental Partners** on a fixed-scope engagement to support a growth decision for their healthcare business. They were evaluating where and how to expand, and needed a clear way to compare options before committing to a site. The goal was to turn a complex decision into a model that showed the financial impact of different operating conditions in a simple, decision-ready format.

I built a three-site expansion model in **Excel** using **financial modeling** methods. The workbook compared three potential sites and tested each one under best, base, and worst cases for both patient volume and staffing. That let Sunrise Dental Partners see how sensitive each location was to changes in demand and labor requirements, instead of relying on a single optimistic forecast.

A key part of the work was structuring the model so it was practical for leadership to use. I organized the inputs, assumptions, and outputs so the business could review the drivers behind each case and quickly see the implications for payback timing. The model was designed to support a real investment decision, not just to present numbers.

```excel
Site            Case     Patient Volume   Staffing   Revenue   Costs   Payback
Site 1          Best     High             Low        input     input   calc
Site 1          Base     Medium            Medium     input     input   calc
Site 1          Worst    Low               High       input     input   calc
Site 2          Base     Medium            Medium     input     input   calc
Site 3          Base     Medium            Medium     input     input   calc
```

The final output compared all **3 sites** and showed which location offered the strongest business case. The chosen site was projected to **pay back in 14 months**, giving Sunrise Dental Partners a clear financial basis for moving forward. That result mattered because it reduced uncertainty around the expansion decision and made the tradeoffs between sites easy to understand.

For me, this project was a good example of how a well-structured financial model can help a healthcare company make faster, better-informed growth decisions.
